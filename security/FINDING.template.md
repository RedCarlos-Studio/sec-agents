# SEC-000: short title

- Status: candidate | confirmed | rejected (reason)
- Severity: Critical | High | Medium | Low | Info
- Confidence: high | medium | low
- Category: CWE-xxx / OWASP Top 10 or API Top 10 item
- Found by: sec-xxx
- Verified by: sec-verifier (date) or pending

## Where
File and line, or endpoint and method, or config and host.

## What
Two or three sentences: what is wrong and why it is a weakness.

## Evidence
Minimal proof. Code excerpt, or request and response with secrets and personal data redacted.

## How to reproduce
Numbered steps using test accounts from SCOPE.md. Harmless marker only.

## Impact
What an attacker gains in this product specifically, and what they need first (unauthenticated, any user, admin).

## Fix
Concrete change for this codebase (file, function, pattern), plus how to confirm the fix.

## Regression test
Test that should be added so this does not come back.
